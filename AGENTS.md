# Product design rules

- Current physical geometry is V68 in `../water-bottle-design/output/prototype-v68/Bureau-73mm-V68-2p2mm-Button-Surround-REVIEW.blend`. Render its actual meshes for website product images; do not regenerate or infer bottle hardware. Preserve its lid-mounted handle, recessed straw, 73 mm body / 249.1 mm closed envelope, recessed body strap mounts, round push button and compact rear hinge with full 220° opening. The recessed mounts supersede the former external collar-lug placement. See `docs/NATIVE_V68_RENDERING.md` and the geometry-integrity evidence.

For a quick continuation, start with `docs/NEXT_SESSION_START.md`. Read `docs/PRODUCT_DESIGN_GUIDE.md` before modifying bottle images, product concepts, colourways, specifications, or marketing copy. It is the consolidated current specification; the historical archive contains rejected versions. Use `design/product-design.json` for references and edition palettes.

- The bottle has **one continuous detachable carry strap**, attached to its side rings with **opening swivel snap hooks at both ends**. The strap clips on and off; do not render permanent closed chain links or sew the strap directly to the bottle. Two visible sides of the same strap are not two separate straps.
- Colourways and kids-friendly colour collections must preserve the approved bottle's physical geometry and proportions. Change colours only unless the user explicitly requests a specific physical modification.
- Preserve both heraldic animals beside the BoP monogram. The latest logo preview removes the shield outline and places a small crown directly on the B. Strap emblems are smaller versions; changing their size does not authorize removing the coat-of-arms animals.
- Follow the latest explicit user correction over earlier concepts or generated images.

- In the half-cut anatomy illustration only, show the strap as one flat cut section with a clean lower end and no return loop. Keep water continuous down to the curved inner floor and illustrate a credible matching screw connection.

- The rear-centre cylindrical head is the spray; the adjacent flush dark hole is not. Preserve clear hood, translucent silicone seal/neck and mirror-polished steel in every colourway.
- Latest hook correction, 15 November 2026: use `docs/v68/SWIVEL_SNAP_HOOK_REFERENCE.png`. The user explicitly rejected the oval wire-loop hook. Use the supplied swivel snap type: round opening jaw, flat tapered trigger body with a small lever/rivet, short swivel connection, separate rounded D-ring and slim folded fabric tab. Attach at the V68 body recesses. This supersedes the old oval-hook/side-lug interpretation and its prohibition on this requested trigger type. Keep all palettes, fabric and exact prints.
- The same reference governs the whole strap: clean plain finely woven webbing, tidy edges and a slim folded same-fabric stitched tab. No dirty/distressed texture, raised geometric jacquard, fraying or oversized leather-like patches. Keep each scene/edition's colour, continuous full-view strap route, cutaway exception and exact vector prints.
- Never degrade image quality between iterations: preserve high-resolution lossless PNG masters, edit from those rather than JPEGs/screenshots/web exports, and export lossless WebP once from the final master. Avoid repeated full-scene generative edits; inspect faces/hands/materials against the earliest sharp approved source, since regeneration can lose detail even without compression.
- Browser logos must use self-contained SVG image exports, not the editable crest's external nested `<use>` chains: Safari/WebKit can lose their masks/clips. Regenerate exports with `scripts/export-browser-safe-crest.py`, preserve ink colours and verify Safari plus Chromium. The header logo is not clickable; the footer back-to-top link remains.

- Latest scene corrections: keep an open mahogany/brass arched ticket window, without bars across the clerk or counter, including lamp animation. Only the two Fig. 03 lid-view strap tabs are unprinted; preserve every other strap print and all bottle crests.

- The round snap jaw wraps the bridge through the native recessed pocket without body intersection. Levers sit **above** the snap body; folded fabric conceals both lower D-ring bars. Check evaluated solid clearance before batch rendering.
- Follow `docs/DESIGN_REVIEW_WORKFLOW.md` for a new design: one affected edition/image type first, then explicit visual sign-off before a full batch. Maintain scripts, references and checks together. CSS/vector changes need no Blender render; use selective hero/section rebuilds.
- Approved 15 November 2026: retain the “Previous proof” translucent lid shader. Dark physical smoke trials were rejected. No large white studio-light blobs, doubled internal reflections or blur. Button touch face stays polished stainless steel; surround retains edition colour.
- Non-cutaway cameras face front at x=0 without horizontal turn. Only the cutaway is oblique. Photographic open lids stop at 90°; the native mechanism still has 220° travel. Preserve the native centred handle.
- Keep the thin cap/body seam, vivid palettes, metrically round Japan suns, the exact approved Canada leaf with upright left leaf and ivory folded tabs, correct Union Jack artwork, and exact strap animals centred at their projected centre height.
- Main scene and lid demo start with straw Up. Keep Close/Open lid and mist controls working, labels apart at phone widths and every leader/mask/emission reprojected to its own view. Main dots stay small beside openings; carry leader touches the strap. Discreet catalogue braille, no cutaway braille, no current prices.
- Cutaway: pickup moves with upper deck/socket; no exposed white connector or body-fixed duplicate. Show only requested metallic exposed threads, double walls/gap, continuous water to native floor and half-clipped crest. Illustrative additions never alter the source .blend or imply manufacturing validation.
- Current corrections were explicitly approved for whole-site publication. Future changes still need the review gate. The packed Blender accessory and safe import steps are in `docs/BLENDER_STRAP_HANDOFF.md`. Preserve `/old/` and never stage unrelated user files.
