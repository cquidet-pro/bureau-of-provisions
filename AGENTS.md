# Product design rules

Read `docs/PRODUCT_DESIGN_GUIDE.md` before modifying bottle images, product concepts, colourways, specifications, or marketing copy. It is the consolidated current specification; the historical archive contains rejected versions. Use `design/product-design.json` for references and edition palettes.

- The bottle has **one continuous detachable carry strap**, attached to its side rings with **opening swivel snap hooks at both ends**. The strap clips on and off; do not render permanent closed chain links or sew the strap directly to the bottle. Two visible sides of the same strap are not two separate straps.
- Colourways and kids-friendly colour collections must preserve the approved bottle's physical geometry and proportions. Change colours only unless the user explicitly requests a specific physical modification.
- Preserve both heraldic animals beside the BoP monogram. The latest logo preview removes the shield outline and places a small crown directly on the B. Strap emblems are smaller versions; changing their size does not authorize removing the coat-of-arms animals.
- Follow the latest explicit user correction over earlier concepts or generated images.

- In the half-cut anatomy illustration only, show the strap as one flat cut section with a clean lower end and no return loop. Keep water continuous down to the curved inner floor and illustrate a credible matching screw connection.

- The rear-centre cylindrical head is the spray; the adjacent flush dark hole is not. Preserve clear hood, translucent silicone seal/neck and mirror-polished steel in every colourway.
- Strap attachment shape is governed by `docs/STRAP_ATTACHMENT_REFERENCE.png`: side lug, angled connector, oval opening clip, short swivel stem, separate rounded rectangular D-ring, stitched tab. Keep this order; do not substitute a broad rectangular clip plate or chunky trigger hook.
- The same reference governs the whole strap: clean plain finely woven webbing, tidy edges and a slim folded same-fabric stitched tab. No dirty/distressed texture, raised geometric jacquard, fraying or oversized leather-like patches. Keep each scene/edition's colour, continuous full-view strap route, cutaway exception and exact vector prints.
- Never degrade image quality between iterations: preserve high-resolution lossless PNG masters, edit from those rather than JPEGs/screenshots/web exports, and export lossless WebP once from the final master. Avoid repeated full-scene generative edits; inspect faces/hands/materials against the earliest sharp approved source, since regeneration can lose detail even without compression.
