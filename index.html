Stainless steel water bottle questions.

What is the bottle made from?

The bottle has a double-wall stainless steel body, a folding steel straw and a wide steel drinking spout. One continuous fabric carry strap clips on and off at both ends. Explore the materials and current CAD specifications.

Can I choose a lid without the mist spray?

Yes. The four lid preferences are Straw & spout, Straw only, Spout only, and Straw, spout & mist. The mist is a cooling spray, separate from the two drinking methods. See how the straw, spout and spray work.

Is the bottle dishwasher safe?

Yes, the bottle is dishwasher safe. Detailed cleaning and care guidance remains in development.

Who are the colour studies for?

The nineteen colour and strap studies are designed for ages 6–10. They all keep the same bottle shape and construction. Colours and final production details remain in development. Explore the colour collection.

How much is it, and where will it ship?

The pre-order price is £39 per bottle. We ship internationally. Shipping charges and delivery dates have not yet been announced.

What happens after I register to pre-order?

Choose a colour, then register your interest through the form. No payment is taken. Ordering details will follow; a production or dispatch date has not yet been announced. This is a product in development, and the images are illustrative design studies.
