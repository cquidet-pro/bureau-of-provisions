A few particulars.

What is the water bottle made from?

The bottle has a double-wall stainless steel design. The folding straw and drinking spout are stainless steel too, so the drinking surfaces that meet your mouth are steel. A clear hinged hood covers the lid.

Which lid should I choose?

Choose Straw & spout for both ways to drink, Straw only for a folding straw, or Spout only for a drinking spout. Straw, spout & mist adds a water spray. All four designs include an air vent and a clear hinged hood. Explore the four lid previews ↓

How does the folding straw work?

The stainless steel straw folds flat beneath the hood. Use the horizontal sliders below to move it from the 45° drinking position to the 180° folded position. The mist lid’s preview ends with the spray in action.

Can I buy the bottle yet?

We’re preparing our first release. You can join the early-access list for updates and leave an optional lid preference. Pricing and a release date have not been announced.
