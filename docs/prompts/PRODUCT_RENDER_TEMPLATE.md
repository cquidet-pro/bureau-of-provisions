# Reusable product image prompt

Read `docs/PRODUCT_DESIGN_GUIDE.md` before filling this template.

## Colour-only studio variant

Use case: precise-object-edit / colour-only product photograph.

Input1: the corrected neutral full-length collection master, the sole geometry/camera reference.
Input2, if supplied: the previous edition, **palette and woven-stripe reference only**. Its older mouthpieces are rejected.

Create edition **{name}**. Change ONLY:
- body coating: {body_hex};
- collar and oval latch coating: {accent_hex};
- the ONE continuous woven strap: {strap_treatment}.

Preserve every other master pixel, silhouette, tall/slim proportions, body height/width, camera, crop, background, lighting, ground plane, metal reflections, fully open clear smooth hood, translucent silicone sealing pad and diagonal neck, low folding straw assembly, squat hourglass spout, rear-centre cylindrical spray head, oval front latch with horizontal inset, and two detachable opening swivel snap hooks. No additional hardware or changed lid poses. No brushed metal or opaque silicone. Colour is not permission to redesign the bottle. Keep a single full product in the identical1024×1536 portrait frame, at the same size as the master. No labels or graphics around it.

Keep body, strap print areas and lower body unprinted. Exact BoP logo, greyhound/leopard and braille are existing SVG layers retained by the website; do not generate replacements. No letters, dots, character faces, badges, accessories, handles, grips, jewels or bumpers. Stripes follow the woven fabric through every fold and the continuous U route. Show the full strap meeting the floor; the cut-strip exception is for anatomy only.

Use realistic polished materials and crisp fibre/coating detail. Save a versioned individual full-resolution PNG plus lossless WebP; record the exact prompt and source. Reject outputs that alter the model or crop.

Attachment/fabric authority: `docs/STRAP_ATTACHMENT_REFERENCE.png`. Preserve side lug → angled connector → oval opening clip → swivel stem → separate rounded rectangular D-ring → neat folded same-fabric stitched tab. Fabric is pristine plain finely woven webbing, with approved edition stripes where applicable; no ornate raised jacquard, dirt, fraying or thick leather-like patch.

Quality lock: use original high-resolution lossless PNG masters as inputs, never a screenshot, JPEG or web-export iteration. No heavy compression between revisions. Preserve detail outside the requested edit. Compare faces/hands/weave/hardware at 100% against the earliest sharp approved image. Reject generative smearing even if the file remains lossless. Export the final WebP losslessly once and record actual dimensions; do not claim interpolation restores detail.

## Local finish correction

Edit target: {approved_asset}. Change ONLY {specified_material_or_texture}. Preserve all geometry, camera, placement, colours, background, logos and all unspecified materials. Steel is mirror-polished; silicone remains translucent; the hood remains clear. Do not interpret reflections as new parts.

## Sectional anatomy

Use the three-quarter original Blender lid reference. Preserve the existing longitudinal cut body, internal straw and water surface. Keep water continuous down to the curved inner floor. Show several complete matching male/female screw turns and a clear exploded gap. Retain the body logo, exact braille and strap emblem as separate SVGs. The visible strap is one cut flat strip, no returning loop, reaching the floor and draping a little forward with a clean cut end. No numeric engineering dimensions are authorised.
